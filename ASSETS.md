# Custom visual assets

All six images were created with the built-in image-generation tool for this project. The final optimized WebP files are saved in `public/images/`. Product visuals are artificial dental models; the interior is an architectural concept. No patient photographs or human figures are used. Original generated PNGs remain in the local generated-images folder. This file records the generation prompts.

## hero

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: premium dental website hero centerpiece. A single oversized anatomically recognizable molar tooth made of pristine glossy white enamel, gently tilted 12 degrees, floating weightlessly in elegant three-quarter perspective. Two beautifully formed curved roots. Sophisticated premium hyperrealistic 3D product render with soft teal and pale mint reflections on enamel, high end octane studio lighting, ambient occlusion, beautiful broad highlights, no toy-like face. Tooth fills 75% of square frame. Genuinely transparent background, no floor or surrounding scene; just the isolated tooth with crisp clean edges, no shadow outside the object. Luxury dental technology campaign aesthetic. No text, no logo, no watermark, no people, no faces, no eyes, no hands, no bodies, no lips, no mouth, no gums. This is an isolated artificial dental display model.
